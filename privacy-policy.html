DelOps ("DelOps", "we", "us", or "our") is a business delivery-operations application that helps organisations synchronize invoices from supported billing software, assign invoices to delivery agents, track delivery outcomes, and view operational reports.

This Privacy Policy explains what information DelOps collects, how we use it, when it is shared, how we protect it, and the choices available to users and organisations.

3. How we use information

  • authenticate users and protect accounts;
  • create and manage organisations and delivery agents;
  • retrieve and reconcile invoices from billing software when an authorised administrator requests a sync;
  • assign invoices to agents and record delivery outcomes;
  • provide reports and operational analytics;
  • process and maintain subscription entitlements;
  • secure the service, investigate failures and prevent abuse; and
  • improve reliability, performance and usability.

4. Billing software integrations

DelOps can connect to supported third-party billing applications. An organisation administrator chooses whether to connect a provider and is responsible for authorising that connection.

DelOps uses supplied credentials only to perform requested integration functions, such as retrieving invoices. Sensitive integration credentials are encrypted before they are stored on the DelOps server and are not intentionally displayed back to the mobile application after storage.

Data retrieved from a connected provider may include business records such as invoice and customer information. That data is processed to provide DelOps functionality to the organisation.

5. How we share information

We do not sell users' personal information.

We may disclose information to service providers that process data on our behalf and to third parties necessary to provide requested features, including:

  • Firebase / Google services for authentication, analytics and crash diagnostics;
  • Google Play and, when available, Apple App Store for subscription purchase and subscription-management services;
  • Connected billing providers selected and authorised by the organisation, such as Swipe and future supported providers; and
  • Infrastructure providers used to host and operate DelOps, including our database and server infrastructure.

We may also disclose information where required by law, legal process, fraud-prevention requirements, or to protect the security and rights of DelOps, our users, or other parties.

6. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards designed to protect personal and business information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or destruction.

Sensitive integration credentials are encrypted before storage on our server. Access to organisation data and integration actions is restricted by authentication, role-based controls and organisation-level access rules.

No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Users are responsible for keeping their authentication credentials and devices secure.

7. Data retention

We retain account, organisation, invoice and delivery information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide DelOps, maintain records requested by the organisation, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and maintain service security and backups.

When information is no longer required, we will delete or anonymise it according to operational retention procedures, subject to legal or legitimate record-keeping requirements.

8. Account and data deletion

DelOps provides an account and data deletion request process. Users can request deletion of their DelOps account and associated personal data by contacting pranabnath9@gmail.com.

Organisation administrators may also request deletion of organisation data. We will verify that the requester is authorised and then delete applicable account and associated personal data unless specific information must be retained by law or for legitimate security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or accounting purposes.

Some records may remain temporarily in secure backups until those backups are rotated according to our retention procedures.

Requests should include enough information for us to identify the relevant DelOps account or organisation. We may request additional verification before processing a deletion request to prevent unauthorised deletion.

Deleting an account does not automatically cancel a subscription purchased through Google Play or the Apple App Store. Subscription cancellation must be completed through the applicable app store account, and DelOps may retain the minimum transaction information required for billing, legal, security, or audit purposes.

9. Children's privacy

DelOps is a business application and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for a child-directed service.

10. Third-party services and links

DelOps may interact with third-party services selected by the organisation. Those services may have their own privacy policies and terms. DelOps is not responsible for the independent privacy practices of third parties outside our control.

11. International processing

Depending on infrastructure and third-party services used to operate DelOps, information may be processed in countries other than the country where the user or organisation is located. We use service providers and safeguards intended to protect information during such processing.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when DelOps features, integrations, legal requirements or data practices change. The "Last updated" date will be updated when material changes are made.
