Billing software integrations

DelOps currently has a tested integration with Swipe. More integrations are planned, including Marg ERP, TallyPrime and Vyapar.

Credentials supplied for supported providers are encrypted before storage on the DelOps server.

How information is shared

We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by infrastructure and service providers required to provide DelOps, including Firebase, app stores, connected billing providers and our hosting/database infrastructure.

Security

We use authentication, role-based access and encrypted storage for sensitive integration credentials. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Retention

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain requested records, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations and maintain security/backups.

Children

DelOps is a business application and is not directed to children.

International processing

Depending on our infrastructure and third-party providers, information may be processed in countries other than the user's location, with safeguards intended to protect the information.

Account deletion

Email DevjoyPN@gmail.com with the phone number used for DelOps and the organisation name. We may ask for verification.

Deleting an account does not automatically cancel a Google Play or Apple App Store subscription. Store cancellation must be handled through the applicable store.

Some information may remain temporarily in secure backups until they are rotated, or where retention is required for legal, security, billing or audit purposes.

Changes & contact

We may update this policy when DelOps features, integrations, legal requirements or data practices change.
